How to get to Domus Duorum from the airport

12 stops

To Casa da Música

At the airport, take the metro, Line E (Violet). Get off at Casa da Música station.

7 min on foot

From the station to the door

Walk for 7 minutes to number 294, Rua dos Vanzeleres.

On foot from the house

5 min on foot

Casa da Música

Designed by the architect Rem Koolhaas and completed in 2005, Casa da Música is one of Porto's most iconic buildings.

7 min on foot

Rotunda da Boavista

Officially Praça Mouzinho de Albuquerque, it has a garden and a central sculpture commemorating the victory of the British-Portuguese alliance over Napoleon's troops.

11 min on foot

Mercado do Bom Sucesso

First opened in 1952, Mercado do Bom Sucesso began as a fresh produce market. Today, better suited to modern life, it is home to food stalls and a variety of places to eat.

26 min on foot

Jardins do Palácio de Cristal

The Palácio de Cristal gardens stand on high ground in Porto and form a very pleasant green space with a fine view over the River Douro. Next door, visit the Museu Romântico.

37 min on foot

Fundação de Serralves

Fundação de Serralves is a cultural institution whose mission is to promote contemporary art. Its grounds hold the Museum of Contemporary Art, extensive gardens, Casa de Serralves and an auditorium.
