Privacy policy

Last updated on 19 November 2026.

The protection and privacy of your personal data matter a great deal to us. We process only the data needed to provide the accommodation and to meet our legal, judicial and tax obligations. This policy explains what data we process, why, for how long, who we share it with and what rights you have, under Articles 13 and 14 of the General Data Protection Regulation (Regulation (EU) 2016/679, the “GDPR”).

Who processes your data

The controller of the personal data processed through this website and at the Domus Duorum accommodation (registrations 94857/AL and 130006/AL) is Isabel Maria Nogueira de Castro Neves, NIF (Portuguese tax number) 190379081, Rua dos Vanzeleres, 294, 4100-482 Porto, who operates both establishments.

To exercise your rights or ask any question about your data, you can contact her:

  • by post, at Rua dos Vanzeleres, 294, 4100-482 Porto, Portugal;
  • by telephone, on +351 961 606 325 (call to a Portuguese mobile network).

What data we process

  • Identification: name, date and place of birth, nationality, identity document and signature.
  • Contact details and address: email, telephone and address.
  • Tax data: tax number and country, when you ask for an invoice.
  • Stay details: dates, booking name and number, number of guests and any requests you make.
  • Proof for exemption from the tourist tax, only if you ask for the exemption: this may include health or disability data.
  • Technical data from your connection to the website and to the check-in page.

The sections below set out, for each case, what data we use, on what legal basis and for how long.

When you visit this website

The website has no forms, does not ask you to register and does not use cookies or any other technology that stores or reads information on your device. It does not use analytics or advertising tools. The fonts and scripts it needs are served by the website itself, without requests to other companies.

As with any website, the server that hosts it receives the technical data of the connection: the IP address, the type of browser and operating system, the page requested, and the date and time. These data are used only to deliver the pages and to keep the website secure. The legal basis is the legitimate interest in making the website available and protecting it from misuse (Article 6(1)(f) of the GDPR).

Website hosting is contracted by the controller from a hosting provider, which processes these data on her behalf, as a processor, and retains them in accordance with its security rules.

When you contact us

If you write to us (by email or WhatsApp) or call us, we process your name, your contact details, the content of your message and anything you choose to share (for example, dates and number of people). We use these data only to reply to you and, where applicable, to prepare your stay. The legal basis is the steps taken at your request prior to entering into a contract (Article 6(1)(b) of the GDPR).

We keep messages for up to one year after the end of your stay, or after the last contact if there is no stay. They are then deleted, unless the law requires us to keep them or they are needed to defend a right.

Email is handled by the SAPO service. WhatsApp messages go through the service of WhatsApp Ireland Limited (Meta group), under its own privacy policy.

Bookings

Bookings made from this website go to Booking.com, which processes your data as an independent controller, under its own privacy policy.

We also accept direct bookings, by telephone, email or WhatsApp.

From Booking.com, or directly from you, we receive the booking data (name, contact details, dates, number of guests and any requests you make) and, for direct bookings paid by bank transfer, the payment details. We use these data to prepare and provide the accommodation. The legal basis is the performance of the contract (Article 6(1)(b) of the GDPR). These data are kept for the duration of the stay and, afterwards, for as long as necessary to comply with legal obligations and to defend rights.

Boletim de alojamento (accommodation registration form for foreign guests)

The law requires accommodation providers to report the arrival and departure of foreign nationals, including citizens of other European Union countries, through SIBA (Sistema de Informação de Boletins de Alojamento, the accommodation registration system), under Articles 15 and 16 of Lei n.º 23/2007, de 4 de julho (Law 23/2007 of 4 July). For this purpose, we ask for the data on the form: name, date and place of birth, nationality, identity document number and issuing country, country and town of residence, dates of stay and signature. Guests under 18 are also registered; their form is signed by a parent or legal guardian.

The legal basis is compliance with a legal obligation (Article 6(1)(c) of the GDPR), not your consent. The forms are kept for one year from the day after the departure is reported, and are then destroyed.

Online check-in

Foreign guests can fill in the registration form on their phone, before or on arrival, at checkin.domusduorumporto.com, which they reach through a link or a QR code we give them. Portuguese citizens do not need to fill it in.

Besides the registration data, the page asks for the booking name and number, an email address and, if you want an invoice, your invoicing details. The email address and booking details are used only to contact you about that stay (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR) and are deleted together with the registration form. The registration data follows the previous section, and the invoicing data the next one.

What you fill in is sent directly to the property’s Google account, where it is stored (Google Drive, Google Sheets and Gmail), and from there the registration is submitted to SIBA. Google processes this data as a service provider. The registration forms, their copies and the emails that accompany them are deleted automatically one year after the departure is reported.

The page is hosted by Netlify (Netlify, Inc.), which receives the technical data from your connection, as the website’s server does. It uses no cookies and no analytics tools; it only stores in your browser the language you choose, so that the page is shown in that language again.

Invoicing

We issue an invoice for every stay. For this, we process your name and, if you request it, your tax number, country and address. The legal basis is compliance with tax obligations, and invoices are kept and reported to the Autoridade Tributária (Portuguese Tax Authority) in the manner and for the period required by tax law.

Tourist tax

The Municipality of Porto charges a tourist tax per night, which we are required to collect. To check who it applies to, we look at the date of birth on your identity document, without taking a copy.

If you ask for an exemption (for example, because you are in Porto for medical treatment or have a disability of 60% or more), you must show us proof. The tax regulation requires us to keep it for one year, in a dedicated file, so that the Municipality can inspect it (Regulamento da Taxa Municipal Turística do Porto, Articles 4 and 7(3)). A document proving the medical appointment or the degree of disability is enough: we do not need diagnoses or medical reports.

Providing it is voluntary and serves only to apply the exemption. Because it may contain health data, we process it only with your explicit consent, given when you hand over the proof (Article 9(2)(a) GDPR); we keep it for the year required by the regulation (Article 6(1)(c) GDPR) and then destroy it.

At the house

Only the controller has access to guests’ data: she handles check-in and cleaning herself and submits the registration forms to SIBA. There are no employees. The companies that store data on her behalf (Google, for online check-in, and whoever provides her invoicing and accounting services) are listed below.

There are no surveillance cameras anywhere on the property.

Consent

The processing described above does not rely on your consent, with one exception: proof of exemption from the tourist tax that contains health data. You may withdraw that consent, but proof already provided must be kept for the year required by the regulation. If we ever wish to use your data for another purpose, we will ask for your consent first; it must be freely given, specific, informed and unambiguous, and you may withdraw it at any time.

How long we keep your data

We keep data only for as long as is strictly necessary for each purpose, with the periods given in each section. Once your stay has ended, we keep only what the law requires us to keep, for the legal periods, or what is needed to defend a right, until that right expires.

Who receives the data

  • the website hosting provider, as described above;
  • Netlify (Netlify, Inc.), which hosts the check-in page;
  • Google (Google Ireland Limited and Google LLC), where the online check-in data is stored;
  • Booking.com, as an independent controller, for bookings made through it;
  • WhatsApp, for messages you send us that way;
  • the competent authorities, through SIBA, in the case of accommodation registration forms;
  • the Autoridade Tributária, in the case of invoices;
  • the Municipality of Porto, when it inspects proof of exemption from the tourist tax;
  • providers of invoicing and accounting services acting on our behalf, in the case of invoices;
  • the courts and other public authorities, only where required by law.

Your data are not sold or used for advertising.

Google LLC and Netlify, Inc. are United States companies, and the check-in data may be processed in that country. Both participate in the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, which the European Commission has recognised as ensuring protection equivalent to that of the European Union (Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2023/1795, Article 45 GDPR). If any other data is processed outside the European Union, this happens only on the basis of an adequacy decision or with another safeguard provided for in the GDPR (Articles 45 and 46).

Your rights

You may request access to your data, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing and portability, and object to processing based on legitimate interest, under Articles 15 to 21 of the GDPR. Simply contact us by any of the means given above. We reply within one month.

If you consider that your data have not been processed in accordance with the law, you may lodge a complaint with the Comissão Nacional de Proteção de Dados (Portuguese Data Protection Authority) (www.cnpd.pt).

Links to other websites

The links to Booking.com, Google Maps («Get directions»), the Registo Nacional de Turismo (National Tourism Register), the Livro de Reclamações (complaints book) and Wikimedia Commons open those websites, which have their own policies. Nothing is loaded from those websites until you click the link.

Cookies

This website and the check-in page do not use cookies and therefore do not display a cookie notice. If this changes, this policy will be updated and your consent will be requested before any cookie that is not strictly necessary.
