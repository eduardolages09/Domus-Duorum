Privacy policy

Last updated on 7 November 2026.

Who processes your data

The controller of the personal data processed through this website and at the Domus Duorum accommodation (registrations 94857/AL and 130006/AL) is Isabel Maria Nogueira de Castro Neves, NIF (Portuguese tax number) 190379081, Rua dos Vanzeleres, 294, 4100-482 Porto, who operates both establishments.

For any question about your data: domusduorum294@sapo.pt or +351 961 606 325 (call to a Portuguese mobile network).

When you visit this website

The website has no forms, does not ask you to register and does not use cookies or any other technology that stores or reads information on your device. It does not use analytics or advertising tools. The fonts and scripts it needs are served by the website itself, without requests to other companies.

As with any website, the server that hosts it receives the technical data of the connection: the IP address, the type of browser and operating system, the page requested, and the date and time. These data are used only to deliver the pages and to keep the website secure. The legal basis is the legitimate interest in making the website available and protecting it from misuse (Article 6(1)(f) of the GDPR).

Website hosting is contracted by the controller from a hosting provider, which processes these data on her behalf, as a processor, and retains them in accordance with its security rules.

When you contact us

If you write to us or call us, we process your name, your contact details, the content of your message and anything you choose to share (for example, dates and number of people). We use these data only to reply to you and, where applicable, to prepare your stay. The legal basis is the steps taken at your request prior to entering into a contract (Article 6(1)(b) of the GDPR).

We keep these messages for as long as necessary to reply to your request and follow up on any stay. They are then deleted, unless the law requires us to keep them or they are needed to defend a right. Email is handled through the SAPO service.

Bookings

Bookings made from this website go to Booking.com, which processes your data as an independent controller, under its own privacy policy.

From Booking.com we receive the booking data (name, contact details, dates, number of guests and any requests you make), which we use to prepare and provide the accommodation. The legal basis is the performance of the contract (Article 6(1)(b) of the GDPR). These data are kept for the duration of the stay and, afterwards, for as long as necessary to comply with legal obligations and to defend rights.

Boletim de alojamento (accommodation registration form for foreign guests)

The law requires accommodation providers to report the arrival and departure of foreign nationals, including citizens of other European Union countries, through SIBA (Sistema de Informação de Boletins de Alojamento, the accommodation registration system), under Articles 15 and 16 of Lei n.º 23/2007, de 4 de julho (Law 23/2007 of 4 July). For this purpose, we ask for the data on the form: name, date of birth, nationality, identity document, country of residence and dates of stay.

The legal basis is compliance with a legal obligation (Article 6(1)(c) of the GDPR), not your consent. The forms are kept for one year from the day after the departure is reported, and are then destroyed.

Invoicing

To issue the invoice, we process your name and, if you request it, your tax number and address. The legal basis is compliance with tax obligations, and invoices are kept and reported to the Autoridade Tributária (Portuguese Tax Authority) in the manner and for the period required by tax law.

Who receives the data

  • the website hosting provider, as described above;
  • Booking.com, as an independent controller, for bookings made through it;
  • the competent authorities, through SIBA, in the case of accommodation registration forms;
  • the Autoridade Tributária, in the case of invoices;
  • other entities, only where required by law.

Your data are not sold or used for advertising.

Your rights

You may request access to your data, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing and portability, and object to processing based on legitimate interest, under Articles 15 to 21 of the GDPR. Simply write to domusduorum294@sapo.pt. We reply within one month.

If you consider that your data have not been processed in accordance with the law, you may lodge a complaint with the Comissão Nacional de Proteção de Dados (Portuguese Data Protection Authority) (www.cnpd.pt).

Links to other websites

The links to Booking.com, Google Maps («Get directions»), the Registo Nacional de Turismo (National Tourism Register), the Livro de Reclamações (complaints book) and Wikimedia Commons open those websites, which have their own policies. Nothing is loaded from those websites until you click the link.

Cookies

This website does not use cookies and therefore does not display a cookie notice. If this changes, this policy will be updated and your consent will be requested before any cookie that is not strictly necessary.

Changes

This policy may be updated. The date at the top shows the last change.
